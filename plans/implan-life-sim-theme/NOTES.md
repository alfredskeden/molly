# Notes

## Decisions (2026-10-12)

- **Life Sim is a theme**, picked in Settings → Theme like the others. Its needs, mood, Wants bubble and moodlets exist only while it's chosen; every other theme looks and behaves exactly as today.
- **Needs drain against the child's own rhythm**, not fixed times:
  - **Sleep**: from the last wake to the start of the next likely sleep (`nextSleep`, the same time as the ring's ghost and the window row). Empty when that time comes.
  - **Feed and diaper**: time since the last entry ÷ the **median gap** between that kind's entries over the last 14 days, taken from gaps in the same window kind (day gaps for the day window, night gaps for the night).
  - The medians are **not polled**: they're computed once per day / night window (when Today turns into Tonight and back), kept for that window, and persisted so an app restart doesn't refetch.
  - **Medicine**: a bar only when the child's Vitamin D reminder is on (`childPrefs(...).vitaminDReminder`), draining from the last Vitamin D over 24 h. Reminder off: the Medicine tile has no bar and no level label.
- **Medicine never counts** in the mood, the Wants bubble or the negative moodlets (a baby on no medicine would otherwise always look upset). A medicine given in the last 30 minutes still earns its positive moodlet.
- **Widgets and the lock-screen notification**: palette, fonts and playful words only, as for every theme. No bars, mood or moodlets.
- **Playful labels off**: bars, the mood pill's colour and face, and the Wants bubble stay; level labels, mood words and moodlet names are hidden (moodlets row hidden entirely), and the centre / toast words go plain as for every theme.

## Defaults chosen during planning (overturn if a step shows they don't work)

- Key `sim`, name **Life Sim**, tagline "Needs drain from green to red. Moodlets included." (the design's), group `little-ones`, **third** in `THEME_KEYS` (after Ribbon), as the design's Theme sheet lists it. `isNew` moves from Ribbon to Life Sim.
- Fewer than 5 gaps of a kind in that window kind → the design's fixed time: feed 3 h, diaper 3.5 h. No `nextSleep` (night, > 6 h awake, outside 6 weeks – 36 months) → sleep 2.5 h from the last wake.
- A gap's window kind is the kind of the window its start falls in (`windowsBefore` / `dayWindow` over the sleeps the `entries` query already holds). Gaps longer than 12 h are dropped (a missed log, not a rhythm).
- The rhythm is stored as `{ feed: ms | null, diaper: ms | null }` per child and window, nothing else: no raw entries in the cache.
- The need colour is quantised to 11 steps (0, 10 … 100: the bar has 10 segments), as hex per scheme, so nothing converts oklch at run time.
- Wants ties (two needs equally low) pick in the order sleep, feed, diaper.
- Screen readers: bars, mood pill, Wants bubble and moodlets are decorative (`aria-hidden` / not accessible); tiles keep their plain spoken text ("Feed, 3 hours ago").
- The stitch along the ring's track (Ribbon) is in Life Sim too: it moves out of `Ornaments` into its own `Look.stitch` slot.
- Life Sim's own words (need levels per kind, mood names, moodlet names, `Way past nap time`, the toast leads) live in a Life Sim word table next to its `needs` slot, not in `Copy`: no other theme has them, and `Copy` would otherwise grow ~25 fields that are null everywhere.

## Design facts (read from `Molly Sim App.dc.html`)

- Tokens: the `sim-day` / `sim-night` blocks at the top of the file. `--bow` / `--bow-shade` are the green used for the toast's face and the Theme row's face icon. `--seg-off` is an empty bar segment.
- Header `--art`: day, two `repeating-linear-gradient`s at 45° and −45° (`oklch(0.88 0.04 215 / .55)`, 2 px every 16 px); night, the same in `oklch(0.25 0.045 260)` with green dots (`oklch(0.85 0.10 145 / .7)`, 43 × 37 at 30 % 40 %) on top.
- Fonts: Fredoka 600 (display: title, tile names, centre number, sheet titles) over Mulish 600–900.
- Need value 0–100. Colour `oklch(0.80 0.16 h)`, h = 25 + 1.2 × value (red → green). Label ink: night the same colour, day `oklch(0.50 0.15 h)`. Level: > 75 / > 50 / > 25 / else → label 0 / 1 / 2 / 3. Running timer: value 82, label `Recharging…`, segments off-colour `oklch(1 0 0 / .2)` on the tile's solid `*-ink` card.
- Level labels: sleep `Bright-eyed` / `Yawning` / `Rubbing eyes` / `Overtired chaos`; feed `Milk drunk` / `Snacky` / `Rooting around` / `HANGRY`; diaper `Fresh bum` / `Probably fine` / `Suspicious smell` / `Code brown`; medicine `Teeth? What teeth` / `A bit gnawy` / `Drool fountain` / `Teething rage`.
- Mood = average need: ≥ 70 `Giggly` (`sentiment_very_satisfied`), ≥ 45 `Content` (`sentiment_satisfied`), ≥ 25 `Fussy` (`sentiment_dissatisfied`), else `Wailing` (`sentiment_very_dissatisfied`). Sleep running: `Dreaming` (`bedtime`). Pill colour = need colour of the average.
- Mood pill: 40 px tall at the ring top (where Ribbon's bow is), the 32 px cap (`surface`, sun / moon in the cap's tone) inside on the left, the face after it. The centre shows the mood word as a chip under the time.
- Wants bubble: bottom-left of the ring box (Ribbon's teddy spot), 56 px `surface` circle with a 2 px ring in the need colour, the wanted tracker's icon over `WANTS`, two small thought dots up-right. Shows the lowest need under 60 (sleep `bedtime`, feed `breastfeeding`, diaper `baby_changing_station`), else `toys` in the full colour; while sleeping `bedtime`. Dims to 0.4 with the editor.
- Moodlets: one 30 px row (overflow hidden) above the window row, only when the tiles show. Positive (green edge, `+n`) for each kind logged ≤ 30 min ago: `Well rested +2`, `Milk drunk +2`, `Fresh bum +1`, `Soothed gums +1`. Negative (red edge) for each need < 25: `Sleepy −2`, `Hangry −3`, `Soggy −2`. While sleeping: `Recharging +3`, `Snug as a bug +1`.
- Tiles: 86 px, a 26 px need-coloured circle with the icon, name, time since (without `ago`), level label in need ink, a 10-segment bar (7 px, 2 px gaps).
- Centre words: awake `Up and about` (muted), sleep need empty `Way past nap time` (need ink), sleeping `Recharging`, feeding `Refuelling`.
- Toast: a filled `sentiment_very_satisfied` face in `--bow` before the text, which starts with the kind's top level label (`Fresh bum! Diaper saved 12:10`); Edit in `--bow`.
- Settings Theme row: a green face icon before "Life Sim". Playful labels row subtitle: `Theme words like “Code brown”`.

  - A gap runs from one entry's **end** to the next one's **start** (a feed's length isn't part of the time between feeds), and the feed need drains from the last feed's end, as the Feed tile counts "since". A change's start and end are its time.

Two causes, both fixed TDD against a seeded two weeks (`needs-seeded.test.ts`: 21:30 – 06:30 nights, three naps, six feeds, five changes), no real logs:
  - A gap was filed under the window its **start** fell in, so the gap from the last feed before bed to the morning feed counted as a day gap and nights had none (3 h fallback). The mood pill and the Wants ring stay the calm 82 while asleep.
  - Seeded on web: 00:02 sleep 28 %, Feed `Snacky`, Diaper `Probably fine`; 04:30 sleep 78 %, Feed `HANGRY` (24 %) as the usual 06:45 feed nears.

Cause: that night's rhythm had been worked out at 00:02 by the first build (old rule: none), persisted under `['rhythm', child, 'night', start]`, and the new build reused it (`staleTime: Infinity`, same key). Paging is not it (`listEntries` collects every page). Fixes: `NEEDS_TUNING.rhythmVersion` (2) is in the key, bumped whenever the rules change; and without a usual gap, while a sleep runs, feed and diaper aren't due before its likely wake (`usually` in `needs.ts`), so too few logged night gaps can't read `HANGRY` mid-sleep either.
